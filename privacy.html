✦ Squeaky privacy policy

Effective: July 6, 2026

Squeaky keeps your data on your device and in your own iCloud. We have no servers, no accounts, and no access to your information. Nothing is sold, shared, or tracked.

What Squeaky stores

The rooms and cleaning tasks you create, and when you complete them. That's it. This data lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud account's private database — which Apple encrypts and which we cannot read, even if we wanted to.

What we collect

Nothing. Squeaky has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data collection.

Purchases

If you buy an upgrade, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple account. We receive no payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove on-device data. Remove iCloud data via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Squeaky. There is nothing for us to delete, because we never had it.

Changes

If a future version of Squeaky changes any of this (for example, a feature that requires a new kind of data), this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships.
