✦ Squeaky

Squeaky is a cleaning scheduler that tells you what to clean today. Rooms get dirty on their own schedule — Squeaky tracks it with per-room dirt meters, so you clean when things are dirty, not when a calendar says you should feel guilty.

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email support@animumrege.com and a human will get back to you.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Squeaky has no accounts and no sign-up — ever. Your data belongs to you.

How does sync work? Squeaky stores your data on your device and syncs it through your own iCloud. Turn iCloud off and Squeaky still works fully on-device.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app, and its iCloud data via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Nothing else exists anywhere.
